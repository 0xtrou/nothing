### Stronger Infrastructure, Healthier Reality

**The Direct Relationship**:

**Strong Infrastructure Produces**:
- Clear identity — you know what you are and what you are not
- Effective exchange — what enters and exits is regulated
- Coherent expression — energy flows through defined channels
- Resilient reality — disturbances are absorbed without collapse

**Weak Infrastructure Produces**:
- Confused identity — boundaries blur, self dissolves into other

**The Health Spectrum**:

| Infrastructure | Reality State |
