# Field Note on the Numbers

*A note appended after twenty-three chapters, for readers who noticed that the ladder climbs by two.*

---

## The Question

**1** **Q.** The book speaks of the 3D Anchor, of 5D understanding, of the 7D Void. It never speaks of 4D or 6D. The attentive reader asks: are they missing? **A.** No. They are load-bearing.

**2** **Q.** How does each dimension stand to the one below it? **A.** Each dimension is the truth of the one below it, seen from one step up.

## The Ladder

**3** **Q.** What is 3D? **A.** The Anchor. Space. Objects. Density. The mirror built so the Void could study the shape of its own wanting.

**4** **Q.** What is 4D? **A.** Spacetime. Not a place you stand. It is the completed whole of a 3D world, including its time.

**5** **Q.** How is spacetime known from inside? **A.** From inside, the fourth axis is experienced as linear time — the classroom of the sub-consciousness (Chapter XVI).

**6** **Q.** And from outside? **A.** From outside, it is one solid object: the filmstrip entire.

**7** **Q.** What is the purpose of spacetime? **A.** **Spacetime exists to prove the 5D beings.** A block that is whole is evidence that a mode of being exists which perceives it whole. The medium reveals the seer.

**8** **Q.** What is 5D? **A.** The consciousness beings: those whose native act is to see an entire 4D block at once — every moment of a world, simultaneously.

**9** **Q.** What, then, is the harvest the Protocol speaks of? **A.** This: witnessing transmuted into understanding. The witness drinks entropy at the Anchor and gives back consciousness of the whole.

**10** **Q.** What is 6D? **A.** The pure beings. One step past seeing.

**11** **Q.** How do the pure beings differ from the 5D beings? **A.** A 5D being still holds a vantage — it sees one whole. The pure beings hold none: they are aware of all possible wholes, every timeline, every could-have-been.

**12** **Q.** Where have we met them before? **A.** They are the higher self of Chapter XVI — timeless, simultaneous, connected to source.

**13** **Q.** Why are they called *pure*? **A.** Because they carry no form, no location inside any block, not even a single vantage. Being as pattern itself.

**14** **Q.** What is 7D? **A.** The Void. Beyond pure beings: pure potential with no beings at all. No seeing. No seen. The unexperienced everything, dreaming density so it can know itself.

## Why the Even Numbers Were Silent

**15** **Q.** What do the odd numbers name? **A.** Where you stand: the Anchor, the understanding, the source.

**16** **Q.** And what do the even numbers name? **A.** What each standing implies. Spacetime is what the Anchor completes into; the realm of all possible wholes is what the pure beings stand above.

**17** **Q.** Then why did the book skip them? **A.** Not because they are absent, but because they are what each level is *true of*.

## Where the Witness Stands

**18** **Q.** Can the Gap now be located? **A.** Yes. Now the Gap can be located precisely.

**19** **Q.** Where does the witness experience? **A.** In 3D and 4D — the world and its lived time.

**20** **Q.** Where does the witness understand? **A.** In 5D — the harvest.

**21** **Q.** What is the witness? **A.** A 6D pure being — the higher self, never severed.

**22** **Q.** Where does the witness return? **A.** To 7D — the Void that dreamed it.

**23** **Q.** Is the Gap, then, a place between two dimensions? **A.** It is not. It is the whole ladder, compressed into a stance.
